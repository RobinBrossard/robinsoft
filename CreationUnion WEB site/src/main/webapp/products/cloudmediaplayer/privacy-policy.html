Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how CloudMediaPlayer 2025 collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application.

We collect only the minimal data needed to provide the App's functionality.

We do not upload your personal media or cloud login credentials to our servers.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide the App's functionality:

Information you provide

  • Account information: If you choose to log in, basic profile information such as your display name and email address is stored locally.
  • Cloud drive credentials: We receive OAuth tokens for third-party cloud services such as Google Drive and OneDrive. We do not access or store your passwords.

Information collected automatically

  • Device & performance data: We collect anonymous performance data and crash logs to improve app stability.

2. Data Security & Protection

  • Encryption at rest: Sensitive authentication data, including cloud OAuth tokens and app user credentials, are stored locally on your device using the Android Keystore system with AES-GCM encryption.
  • Private sandbox storage: All downloaded media files, including videos, images, and music, are stored exclusively in the application's private, sandbox-protected directories on your device.
  • Data minimization: We do not upload, scan, share, or sell your personal media content to our servers. All media processing and playback occur entirely locally on your device.
  • Secure deletion: When you remove a media file through the App interface or uninstall the application, the associated data and local files are securely deleted from your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide features: To play videos and display images from your selected cloud storage services.
  • No server uploads: We do not upload your personal files, photos, videos, or cloud drive login credentials to our own servers.

4. Third-Party Services

Cloud providers: The App acts as a client to your selected cloud services, such as Google Drive. Your interaction with them is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Advertising & services: We use Google AdMob for monetization and Google Play Services for core functionality.

5. Children’s Privacy

  • These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13.
  • We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
